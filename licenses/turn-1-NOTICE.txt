Qwen3-ASR-1.7B, Alibaba Cloud Qwen team. Apache License 2.0. https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-ASR-1.7B
Qwen3-ASR-0.6B, Alibaba Cloud Qwen team. Apache License 2.0. https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-ASR-0.6B
Qwen3-ASR code: https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-ASR and the PyPI package qwen-asr 0.0.6. Apache License 2.0.
  p99turn1/turn1.py contains a batched re-implementation of the forward pass of the Qwen3-ASR audio encoder,
  derived from qwen_asr/core/transformers_backend/modeling_qwen3_asr.py. Changes: batching over windows of at
  most one attention block; hidden states returned at three depths.
